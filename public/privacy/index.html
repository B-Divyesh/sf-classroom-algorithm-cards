Privacy

Read the privacy policy

You can use the kit without an account or payment. Do not enter student names or classroom information in shared print filenames.

Kit settings

Your normal kit settings are stored in this browser so they are available when you return. Demo settings use a separate browser space and are removed when you choose Start for real.

Requests and tracking

The app does not send your kit settings to another service. It does not load analytics, advertising, or third-party scripts.

Offline files

After the first visit, your browser can keep public app files in its offline cache. The cache contains the app files, not your printed kit or classroom names. You can clear it through your browser’s site-data controls.

Use with children

This is a teacher or adult facilitator tool. Do not enter student names or personal information into browser print dialogs or shared filenames.
