Plain-language privacy

We do not collect your classroom.

Classroom Algorithm Cards runs in your browser. It has no accounts, analytics, ads, forms, or student profiles. Your kit choices are not sent to us or saved between visits.

What the site stores

The site may store its own public files in your browser’s offline cache. That lets an already-opened kit continue to work if the connection drops. This cache contains the app—not your selections, documents, or student information—and you can remove it through your browser’s site-data controls.

What reaches the server

Like any website host, our hosting infrastructure may receive short-lived technical request information such as an IP address, requested path, browser type, and timestamp for security and reliable delivery. We do not add tracking scripts or combine this information into classroom profiles.

Children

This tool is for a teacher or adult facilitator. Children do not need to use the website, submit information, or create an account. Please do not put student names or personal information into print dialogs or shared filenames.

Questions

You can open an issue in the project’s public source repository. Last updated: August 27, 2026.
